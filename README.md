### 👨‍💻 What I do

I'm a backend & systems engineer building **production infrastructure for railway safety and monitoring** — the kind of systems that run 24/7 on edge hardware in the field and can't afford to fall over.

Day to day that means:

- **Real-time signaling & sensor diagnostics** — parsing binary packets off serial links, GSM/LTE modems, and RTUs, then turning noisy field data into actionable alarms.
- **Distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) pipelines** — signal processing + ML to tell an elephant apart from a passing train.
- **Time-series data at scale** — multi-TB PostgreSQL (partitioning, query planning, HA), plus VictoriaMetrics + Grafana for monitoring the monitors.
- **Linux daemons & edge systems** — systemd, IRQ/serial debugging, iptables, Docker networking, and license enforcement bound to the hardware itself.
- **Concurrent Go services** — gRPC, WebSockets, and MQTT/Kafka/NATS message plumbing holding it all together.

I like working close to the metal — where a bug is a race condition or a dropped packet, not a missing semicolon.

### 🌱 What I'm into

- **Systems & low-level programming** — currently learning **Zig** and poking at embedded (RP2040, `no_std`).
- **Signal processing & ML/DL** — mostly through vibe coding, but the models ship. 🎛️
- **Databases done right** — Postgres internals, HA, and squeezing seconds off slow queries.
- **Content creation** — writing & videos on backend and production engineering.
